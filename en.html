Who We Are

JM-ideal (JML Electric Appliance Limited) is an authorized Midea agent for West Africa, headquartered in Shunde, Foshan, Guangdong — the heart of China's home appliance industry. Our founder brings nearly 30 years of experience in AC manufacturing, quality control and international trade.

We do not run our own factory. Instead, we integrate the full product lines of leading manufacturers — air conditioners, refrigerants, installation accessories, spare parts and professional tools — to deliver a true one-stop supply solution for our partners. As an authorized Midea West Africa agent, we guarantee genuine Midea products, factory-direct pricing and reliable after-sales support.

Our team responds fast on WhatsApp and supports partners with installation guidance, spare parts availability and transparent shipping via Tema and Tincan ports.
