🔒4. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your personal data:

  • SSL/TLS Encryption: All data transmitted between your browser and our website is encrypted using HTTPS/SSL
  • Secure Hosting: Website hosted on GitHub Pages with Cloudflare CDN, providing DDoS protection and security patches
  • Access Control: Only authorized JML personnel have access to customer data, and access is limited to what is necessary for their role

While we strive to protect your personal data, please note that no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security, but we are committed to protecting your data to the best of our ability.

If you do this, however, you may have to manually adjust some preferences every time you visit a site.
