Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions from distributors, wholesalers and JIJI sellers across Ghana, Nigeria and West Africa.

What is the minimum order quantity (MOQ)?

+

Split AC: 20 sets per model. Commercial / VRF: 1 set. Parts & accessories: 5–100 pcs depending on item. Mixed container loading is available — combine multiple models in one 20ft or 40ft HQ container to reduce your per-unit shipping cost.

What payment methods do you accept?

+

We accept T/T bank transfer (30% deposit, 70% before shipment), L/C at sight for large orders, and Western Union / MoneyGram for sample and small orders. For regular customers in Ghana and Nigeria, local GHS / NGN cash on delivery (COD) may be arranged through our bonded warehouse partners in Tema and Tincan.

How long is the delivery time?

+

Stock items: 3–7 days after deposit. OEM / white-label orders: 15–25 days. Sea freight to Tema (Ghana): ~28–35 days. To Tincan (Nigeria): ~30–40 days. We can also ship by air or express for urgent spare parts (3–7 days).

Are the units 220V / 50Hz suitable for West Africa?

+

Yes. All our split, portable and commercial units are configured for 220V / 50Hz / 1PH — the standard across Ghana, Nigeria, Togo, Benin, Ivory Coast and most of West Africa. VRF systems are 380V / 3N / 50Hz. We do not sell 110V / 60Hz units for the North American market.

What warranty do you offer?

+

1-year compressor warranty on all Midea-brand units. 1-year parts warranty on indoor/outdoor PCBs, fan motors and remote controls. We provide free spare parts (PCB, remote, capacitor, sensor) equal to 1% of order value for after-sales support. Extended warranty available for large distributors.

Do you provide installation accessories?

+

Yes. Each split AC set includes basic installation accessories (remote control, battery, drain pipe, wall plate, screws). For installers and shops, we offer a complete 3M installation kit with insulated copper pipe, brass fittings, universal remote, indoor/outdoor PCB, rotary compressor and fan motors — hard-to-source parts that local technicians in Accra and Lagos struggle to find.

Can I get white-label / OEM packaging with my own brand?

+

Yes, white-label packing is optional. We can apply your brand logo, custom carton design and user manual in English or French. MOQ for OEM is typically 100 sets per model. This is ideal for JIJI top sellers and distributors who want to build their own brand recognition in the local market.

Do you have local stock in West Africa?

+

We work with bonded warehouse partners in Tema (Ghana) and Tincan (Nigeria) for fast-moving models and spare parts. Local stock means you can receive goods in 2–5 days instead of waiting 30+ days by sea. Contact us for current stock availability and local GHS/NGN pricing.

I sell on JIJI — can you support my online store?

+

Absolutely. We provide JIJI sellers with: high-quality product photos and videos for listings, competitive wholesale pricing with quantity discounts, drop-shipping support from bonded warehouse, and after-sales spare parts supply. Top JIJI sellers in Accra and Kumasi already source from us. Send your JIJI store link for a special partner rate.

Which refrigerant do your units use?

+

Most split and portable units use R410A. Newer models (including P9 cassette) use R32 — more environmentally friendly with lower global warming potential. Solar AC models use R410A. VRF systems use R410A. All refrigerants comply with international standards and are available for purchase as spare consumables.

Can I order a sample before placing a bulk order?

+

Yes. Sample orders of 1–2 units are accepted at sample pricing (slightly higher than bulk). Sample cost can be deducted from your first bulk order of 20+ sets. Samples ship by express air (DHL/FedEx, 5–7 days) or can be collected from our Shunde office.

How do I become an authorized distributor in my country?

+

We offer exclusive or semi-exclusive distribution rights for qualified partners in West African countries. Requirements: minimum annual purchase volume, local after-sales capability, and a physical showroom or warehouse. Benefits: protected pricing, priority stock allocation, marketing support, and joint promotion. Contact us with your company profile for evaluation.
